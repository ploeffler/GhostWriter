05 · Reviewing the draft

Read, improve, decide

After the additions, the complete affected section is reviewed again.

Read it in context

Do the sequence, transitions and narrative voice work? Does the section repeat something from another chapter? Can readers distinguish remembered experience from its present-day interpretation?

Preserve uncertainty

Uncertain dates are identified. Gaps are not filled with invented events or reactions.

Understand the status

The example ends with a saved, revised working draft. Permission to write or a saved change does not replace the author’s final approval.

The process at a glance

Tell → explore → organise → draft → read → refine the voice → review. The author can add material and change direction at any point.
