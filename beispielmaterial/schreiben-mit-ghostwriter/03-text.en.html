03 · An additional text

Bring work you already have

A text developed in another AI conversation can also serve as source material.

Introduce the text

The author supplies a draft already developed elsewhere. It adds to the memories and answers from the current conversation.

Clarify meaning and details

The author explains which statements reflect their experience and what a particular change means. These clarifications guide the next draft.

Developing a text brought from elsewhere

Fictional dialogue example · Illustrates the process; these are not original quotes. The sample prose is fictional too.

Author

Here’s a text I worked on in another chat. Some of it gets closer than our first draft.

GhostWriter

The text says everything is easier today. Do you mean the tasks themselves, or the way you see yourself?

Author

More the way I see myself. The tasks are still difficult.

GhostWriter

Then let’s bring out that distinction: everyday life remains demanding, but you judge yourself differently.

Develop it within the book

The additional text and confirmed details form the basis of a new narrative. Transitions and connections to other chapters are updated too.

What changes

Earlier work becomes part of the development. The author can continue without having to tell every thought again.
