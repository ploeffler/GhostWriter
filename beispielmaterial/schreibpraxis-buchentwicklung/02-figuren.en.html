02 · People and conflicts

Who cannot let it go?

The setting leads to the question of a character who has something to lose there.

GhostWriter

Whose perspective would you like to follow when the dead man is found?

Author

A woman who restores old photographs. Mara, perhaps in her late thirties. She has come back after years away to take over her mother’s business.

GhostWriter

How does Mara become involved in the death without simply taking over the police investigation?

Author

She is commissioned to restore photographs for an exhibition about the landing. She recognises her mother in one picture. Her mother has always claimed she was not there that evening.

GhostWriter

What does Mara initially want to know from her mother?

Author

Why she lied. Her mother shuts her out. Mara wants to believe her, but she does not want to be brushed off like a child again.

GhostWriter

Who can challenge Mara if she reads too much into a photograph?

Author

Her old school friend Jonas is a police officer. He takes her seriously, but he does not simply share the case files. And the missing woman has a brother who has been looking for an answer for years.

Characters grow through relationships

Mara gains an occupation, a reason to return and a personal conflict. Her mother, Jonas and the brother play different parts in the story. Their roles emerge from the material already developed.

The example does not begin by collecting complete profiles of every character. It first clarifies who is needed for the central tension.
