01 · Setting and starting idea

A place with something to hide

An interest in small places provides the starting point for the plot.

GhostWriter

Where might that closeness be especially noticeable? Do you have a place in mind?

Author

A fictional small town on an Austrian lake. The present day. In autumn, when the tourists have gone.

GhostWriter

What disturbs the peace there? An event or a discovery we could begin with?

Author

A man is found dead at the old ferry landing. He wanted to demolish it and build holiday apartments there.

GhostWriter

The demolition could affect several people. Why is this landing more than an old structure?

Author

A young woman disappeared there thirty years ago. Nobody talks about it. Perhaps it was an accident back then.

GhostWriter

Should the story be told entirely in the present, or would you also like to show those earlier events directly?

Author

The present for now. The past should emerge gradually, rather than becoming a second novel in between.

Setting and time frame

Fictional Austria, present day, autumn: these details shape the atmosphere and research needs. The landing connects the current death with an unresolved story from the past.

A possible accident remains a suggestion by the fictional author. The dialogue does not turn it into an established backstory.
