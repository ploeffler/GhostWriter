04 · Voice and scope

How should the book read?

The story developed so far helps shape relevant questions about viewpoint, language and length.

GhostWriter

Mara should be able to misjudge her mother. Would you therefore like to stay close to Mara’s perception, or should readers sometimes know more than she does?

Author

Close to Mara, but not in the first person. Past tense. Readers should discover what happened alongside her.

GhostWriter

You said suspenseful, but not brutal and not just cosy. What might Mara’s view of the place sound like?

Author

Closely observed and sometimes dryly funny. But the missing woman should not just be an object in a puzzle. We should feel that she had a life.

GhostWriter

Would you prefer a short novel, or room for relationships and several false leads?

Author

A standard novel, perhaps 250 to 300 pages. A standalone book for now. In German. My name is Lena Berger; let’s use that as the author name here.

GhostWriter

As working titles, “Das Alibi am See” could name the plot, “Was der Anleger verschwieg” could foreground the setting, or “Ein Bild vom Schweigen” could connect Mara’s occupation with the family conflict. Which direction appeals to you?

Author

“Ein Bild vom Schweigen”. As a working title. The final title can come later.

Basic details from the conversation

German, a standalone crime novel, approximate length, a viewpoint close to Mara, past tense and a provisional title: these details are clarified in relation to the story. Page count is a wish, not a promise about the final layout.

The name Lena Berger is fictional too. The German working title “Ein Bild vom Schweigen” roughly means “A Picture of Silence”. These style preferences are initial guidelines, not a style profile analysed from a reference text.
