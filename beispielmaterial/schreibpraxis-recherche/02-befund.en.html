3 · Finding and proposal

Make the claim more precise

A useful revision explains which overstatement it removes.

GhostWriter

The sentence conflates prediction and early warning. The conclusion about “every” earthquake also goes beyond the source. I suggest: “Earthquake early warning systems detect an earthquake that has already started. Under suitable conditions, they can issue a warning before stronger shaking reaches a more distant location.” [Q1]

Author

That is more precise. But I do not want readers to assume they can always react in time, wherever they are.

GhostWriter

Then let us add an explicit limit: “This does not guarantee a timely warning at every location.” We would remove the original final sentence. The source describes a limit related to distance; I will not add a specific number of seconds without reference to a particular system.

From finding to revision
“reliably predict”Replace with detection of an earthquake that has already started.
“enough time before every…”Remove; a warning at every location is not guaranteed.
New figures or success ratesDo not add: this task has not established sufficient evidence for them.

Proposed change, not yet applied
