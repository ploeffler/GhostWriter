04 · Editing together

One possible version

This version combines selected phrases with feedback from the fictional person. It is another proposal.

Simulated editing result

A letter has been lying on my desk for a week. Written, signed, in an envelope and addressed. Apart from the stamp, everything is ready. Unfortunately, that is where the postal service insists on completeness.

I pass the corner shop several times a week. While I am out, I do not think about the letter. At home, I immediately remember it and give myself a hard time. I usually do similar things for my neighbour straight away. Why that feels easier, I do not know.

I wrote myself a reminder note. Now there are two things on the desk. Tomorrow my partner is supposed to remind me before we go shopping. I do not know yet whether that will help. The letter, at any rate, is not on its way.

The ending stays open

The letter has not been posted. Revision makes the text more coherent without assigning the person a success or an explanation.
