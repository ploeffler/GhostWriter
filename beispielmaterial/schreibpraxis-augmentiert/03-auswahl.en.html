03 · The person chooses

What sounds like me?

The proposals are on the table. Now the example person decides which phrases fit and where an exaggeration goes too far.

One possible response from the still fictional example person:

I like the line about the postal service. I recognise my voice in “almost everything a letter needs”, too. But “our joint project” sounds too grand for me. And I want to keep it clear that I cannot explain the difference with my neighbour.

The person contributes again: choosing, rejecting an exaggeration and keeping an open question open. The following result is an additional proposal, not a decision made on behalf of the real author.

Feedback is a contribution too

Choosing, rejecting, setting limits: the person shapes the voice of the next version. Their uncertainty remains part of the story.
