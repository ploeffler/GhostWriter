1 · The memory

“Haven’t we researched this before?”

Remembering an earlier book is a useful start — but not a reference yet.

Author

Haven’t we already researched earthquakes for one of my books? What did I write there?

GhostWriter

I can search the texts and research material I actually have access to. Do you remember a book or another keyword besides “earthquake”?

Author

Probably “Wenn der Boden sich bewegt” [When the Ground Moves]. It was about whether a warning can be issued before an earthquake. For this example, you have the manuscript and my research folder for that book. The older travel book is not included.

GhostWriter

Then I will search the supplied manuscript and that research folder for the German terms “Erdbeben”, “Frühwarnung” and “Vorhersage”. The travel book is outside the search. Not finding something here would not mean you never wrote about it elsewhere.

Search scope in this example

One fictional manuscript and one fictional research folder are available. Another book is explicitly missing.

A requirement for real work

The following findings are constructed for the simulation. In a real task, the books and material must be accessible; an assistant’s recollection is no substitute for a search.

Q1 · Expert source actually consulted

Swiss Seismological Service (SED) at ETH Zurich: Can earthquakes be predicted? In particular, the introduction and the “Earthquake early warning” section. No publication date displayed; accessed on 10 November 2026.

Institutional expert information, not an original research study. A factual check for this example, not a complete literature review.
