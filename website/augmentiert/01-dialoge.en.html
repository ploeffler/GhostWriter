01 · Five question rounds

Turning a statement into material

Three question variants per round, one shared answer: all drafts receive the same information.

How to read this: Each round presents three possible questions. The shared author answer applies to all three variants. This is a deliberate simplification: all three drafts receive the same information. Real conversations would develop differently depending on the follow-up questions.

Round 1 — Where exactly does it get stuck?

Accompanying assistant — variant written here

If the letter is already written, what step is still missing before you can put it in the postbox?

Gemini — simulated variant

What exactly does “finished” mean for this letter? Where does its journey from the desk to the postbox stop?

Perplexity — simulated variant

Is getting to the postbox really the only thing left, or does something else need to happen first?

Example person — fictional shared answer

It is signed, in an envelope and addressed. It needs a stamp. The postbox alone would not be enough.

What we have learned

The visible standstill involves a specific unfinished intermediate step. This does not yet explain its cause.

Round 2 — What opportunities arise in everyday life?

Accompanying assistant — variant written here

Where could you get the stamp, and how does that fit into your usual routes?

Gemini — simulated variant

What opportunities were there to get a stamp this week? What actually happened?

Perplexity — simulated variant

Is there somewhere nearby where you could buy a stamp?

Example person — fictional shared answer

At the corner shop two streets away. I pass it several times a week. When I am out, I do not think about the letter. When I get home, it is there to remind me.

What we have learned

There is somewhere to buy the stamp. Remembering at home and acting while out are separate; why this happens remains open.

Round 3 — How does it become a judgement about yourself?

Accompanying assistant — variant written here

What goes through your mind when you come home and see it on the desk again?

Gemini — simulated variant

How do you experience that moment when you return and realise another opportunity has passed?

Perplexity — simulated variant

What do you say to yourself about the letter still lying there?

Example person — fictional shared answer

That it is ridiculous. Such a small thing. Then I tell myself I am just lazy. That is about the only task I reliably complete with this letter: giving myself a hard time.

What we have learned

“Lazy” is the fictional person’s reported self-judgement. The text may present it as such; it should not turn it into an established explanation.

Round 4 — Is there a difference in a similar situation?

Accompanying assistant — variant written here

What is it like when you have to do a small errand like this for someone else?

Gemini — simulated variant

Can you think of a comparable situation in which buying or posting something felt easier?

Perplexity — simulated variant

Do other people’s letters also sit there if you are supposed to take them along?

Example person — fictional shared answer

If my neighbour gives me something to take, I usually deal with it straight away. I cannot tell you why that is different. I do not want to invent an explanation just to make the story neat.

What we have learned

A difference has been reported. Interest, responsibility, pressure or a particular psychological cause have not thereby been confirmed as explanations.

Round 5 — What has been tried, and what is only a plan?

Accompanying assistant — variant written here

What have you tried so far to take the letter with you, and is there a next attempt you actually intend to make?

Gemini — simulated variant

What help have you already tried? And what would you try next, without knowing yet whether it will work?

Perplexity — simulated variant

Have you already made yourself a reminder or asked someone to remind you before your next trip out?

Example person — fictional shared answer

I wrote myself a note. It is now lying next to the letter. Tomorrow I am going shopping with my partner. I have asked him to remind me about the letter before we leave. I do not know yet whether that will help.

What we have learned

The note is an attempt actually reported within the fiction. The partner’s reminder is a plan. The letter has still not been posted.
