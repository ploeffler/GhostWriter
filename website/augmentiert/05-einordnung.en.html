05 · What works together

Experience, proposal and judgement

The example shows a way of working. It neither ranks providers nor claims to be an actual model comparison.

Recommendation: Show the three variants as possible ways of talking and writing, and make the person’s subsequent selection a visible part of the walkthrough.

  • Shared facts: All variants received the same starting situation and the same five answers. The stamp, corner shop, self-reproach, neighbour and previous attempts remain present.
  • Differences: The questions approach things differently; the drafts differ in rhythm, punchlines and compression. This says nothing about the actual quality of a provider.
  • Check: No cause is invented and no posting is claimed. Humorous phrases are wording proposals; the person decides whether they fit their voice.
  • Human contribution: Initial experience, concrete answers, uncertainty, judgement of proposals and decisions about the version.
  • AI contribution: Questions, organisation, wording suggestions and editorial connections.
  • Limit: This script demonstrates a possible process. A real provider comparison would require actual conversations with a documented starting assignment.

The shared process

Starting situation → questions → answers → drafts → human selection → edited version. At every transition, the person can correct things and change direction.
