Opening

An idea becomes a crime novel

It begins with a wish. Through conversation, a story, characters and the first decisions for a book take shape.

Author

I want to write a book.

GhostWriter

Is there something you already want to tell, or a kind of book you would like to write?

Author

A crime novel. I like small places where everyone knows everyone, but everyone still has something to hide.

GhostWriter

What particularly appeals to you: the puzzle, the people behind the secrets, or the investigation?

Author

The people. But by the end I do want to understand how everything fits together. No serial killer and no constant violence.

GhostWriter

Then let’s look for a small circle of people whose relationships drive the mystery. Who do you imagine reading the story?

Author

Adults who enjoy a suspenseful novel in the evening. Not too brutal, but not just cosy either.

The direction becomes clearer

“A book” becomes a crime novel for adults, a close-knit community and an initial sense of tone. The question about its focus grows out of the interest in local secrets.

No title or finished plot is needed yet. The dialogue starts with what already interests the person.
