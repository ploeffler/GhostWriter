02 · A later section

Finding a place in the book

New material does not all belong in the same place. First, the purpose of this section is clarified.

Define its purpose

A later chapter explores how past experiences come to be seen differently. Other parts of the book develop what this means for the present.

Use existing material

Previously shared memories and new answers are brought together. Open questions remain visible. This material provides the basis for the section.

From proposal to manuscript

A draft is first shown in the conversation. Following the instruction to write it, it is saved as manuscript text. The other sections of the chapter have not been written yet.

What changes

The new section has a clear purpose and a place in the book. Collected material and manuscript text remain distinguishable.
