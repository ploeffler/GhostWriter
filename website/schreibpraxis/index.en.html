From conversation to your own text

Writing with GhostWriter

A book grows through conversation. This example shows how memories, an additional text and the author’s feedback become a revised narrative.

The route through this example

We follow the work on a first chapter and, later, on an individual section. You can see how material is gathered, drafted and refined.

Based on real work

The steps come from a book project. Personal content and project details have been omitted or abstracted. The account is condensed and does not reproduce a conversation word for word.

The principle

The author contributes experiences, chooses the direction and judges the text. GhostWriter asks questions, organises material and develops drafts.
