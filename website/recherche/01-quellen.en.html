2 · The source

What does the source support?

A suitable source must address the particular claim made in the text.

GhostWriter

The Swiss Seismological Service at ETH Zurich distinguishes earthquake prediction from early warning in its FAQ. It explains that early warning processes measurements of an earthquake that has already started. A warning can reach more distant areas before the shaking arrives there. [Q1]

Author

Is that a research study? And does it let me keep the word “reliably”?

GhostWriter

It is an expert overview from the seismological service, not an individual original research study. It suits the basic distinction. However, it does not establish an overall reliability rate or a promise for every location. For that, we would need to examine a particular system and its performance data.
