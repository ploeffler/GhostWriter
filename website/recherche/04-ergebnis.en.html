5 · Result and limits

What was checked — and what remains open?

A review note makes the scope of the work clear.

Review note for the simulated passage
Text reviewedThe full three-sentence original paragraph from stage 1 and the revised version from stage 4.
FindingPrediction and early warning were conflated; the conclusion promised warning time for every strong earthquake.
RevisionClarified the terms, removed the blanket promise, added a limit and a working reference.
EvidenceOne institutional expert source actually consulted; accessed on 10 November 2026. No systematic literature review.
Open itemsThe separate explanation of prediction; a consistent citation style; current technical evidence if specific systems are discussed.
ResultEditorial review passed with notes for this sample passage. No judgement about the entire book, no saving and no formal approval.

Apply this to your own text

Define the claim, read a suitable source, separate findings from proposals, review the whole paragraph and record the author’s decision.
