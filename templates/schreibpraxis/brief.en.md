# Brief: a new Writing practice episode

Create a local bilingual HTML walkthrough for GhostWriter. Read `templates/schreibpraxis/README.en.md` and the series rules first. Use its HTML/CSS templates and the existing episodes as references. Do not redesign existing public pages incidentally.

Before drafting, establish:

- topic, audience and what readers should be able to understand afterwards;
- episode title, a few main stages and intended tile position in the overview;
- actual sources, abstracted content and permitted simulation scope;
- human contributions and decisions that should be visible in the process.

Derive follow-up questions from what has been said. Do not ask for known details as if unknown. Distinguish proposals, author decisions, open questions and results. Never invent successful saving, provider calls or approvals. Clearly identify simulated dialogues and drafts.

Deliver German and English, versioned content, a generator and maintenance notes. The logo leads to the matching overview; the episode title leads to its opening. Series name only beneath GhostWriter, simulation notice directly below the menu. Add the tile to both overviews; READMEs and homepages retain text links to the overview.

Run the guide’s local checks and show the draft. Upload or publish only as explicitly authorised by the current user request. Report the actual verification and publication status.
