# Your book. Step by step.

[Deutsch](WEBSITE-START.md) · English

This program documentation takes you from setup to an edition. Work in conversation with your AI assistant; GhostWriter keeps the outline, research, text and approvals together.

## Where would you like to start?

- **New here?** [Set up and start](USER-GUIDE.en.md): repository, connection, first book and first chat.
- **Understand the process:** [Book lifecycle](BOOK-LIFECYCLE.en.md), [writing and revision](CREATION-CYCLE.en.md), [reviewing text](QUALITY-ASSURANCE.en.md).
- **Keep your work:** [Saving and merging](SAVING.en.md), [conversation backups](SESSION-BACKUP.en.md).
- **Shape your book:** [Images and references](IMAGES-AND-REFERENCES.en.md), [settings and output](SETTINGS.en.md).
- **Something went wrong?** [Troubleshooting](TROUBLESHOOTING.en.md) or [help and commands](HELP.en.md).

## See how it works

The existing example pages demonstrate setup, book development and programming with AI. Open them using the examples navigation above. They are examples, not additional requirements for your book.

## Available and planned

The guides describe the existing program. Planned parts are explicitly marked **planned** or **not yet implemented**. In particular, [media layout](MEDIA-LAYOUT.en.md) describes requirements, not available layout controls. [Images and references](IMAGES-AND-REFERENCES.en.md) also explains the limits of passing files to image services.

Saving, merging, content approval and publication are separate steps. The displayed program version applies to this documentation; your book may use a different update level. Use **Data** or **Update** in the chat to check it.
